# Creator Fund and sustainable operation

## Confirmed direction

Desk Crawler's MVP is exclusively for TRMNL users, with a free companion website and public sample preview. The TRMNL Creator Fund is the intended sole revenue source. No player subscriptions, purchases, ads, paid resurrection or reward boosts are planned.

A saved installation is required once to activate a hero. Removing installations afterwards preserves the hero and passive progress. A premium experience means polished setup, readable e-ink, authored content, reliable progression and support.

## What the fund currently rewards

Checked 2026-10-03: the August 2026 report describes relative rewards based on age on live playlists, playlist presence and displayed impressions. It states a minimum of 50 connections (installs + forks) and warns that weights and thresholds can change. This is not a fixed payment per download. [Official August report](https://trmnl.com/blog/creator-fund-08-2026)

The same report's plugin-author allocation was $3,979.43 across community plugins for that payout period, not a forecast for this game. The fund announcement also lists a category exclusion. Do not infer eligibility for a Third Party game from general plugin wording or marketplace approval. [Fund announcement](https://trmnl.com/blog/creator-fund)

## Evidence before launch claims

V10 requires a dated record of:

- Whether this Third Party integration and selected game/category qualify under current rules.
- Owner registration, payout setup, current threshold and any other qualifying conditions.
- Which platform-reported connection/playlist/impression figures are available and what they mean.
- Expected incremental costs at the measured load, including a zero-payout case.

Store redacted findings in the [evidence checklist](evidence/README.md). Research and a prepared submission do not authorize contacting TRMNL, changing payout accounts or publishing.

Eligibility may be proven before launch while the payout threshold remains unmet. Fifty connections is a financial qualification assumption to recheck, not a beta quota or launch prerequisite. If this game is ineligible, bring the financial launch decision to Barry before proceeding; do not silently add billing or change channels.

## Economics and product incentives

Use actual payout history when available. Before that, budget for zero revenue rather than estimate a universal amount per install or impression. Compare realized payouts with incremental service spend and support effort using [operations](operations.md).

Track activated heroes separately from currently polling installations: disconnected heroes still incur simulation costs. Pending heroes do not earn rewards but still consume bounded storage/index reads. Exclusivity aligns acquisition with the intended revenue channel; it does not guarantee cost coverage.

Improve retention by making the screen useful and delightful. Respect mixed playlists, mashups, slower refresh and Sleep Mode. Never create reward incentives for polling, manipulate impressions, ask users to disable sleep or occupy every playlist slot, or add forced web visits to satisfy a revenue metric.

D46 adds optional [Desk keepsakes](playlist-retention.md): a permanent cosmetic collection claimed with a weekly screen code, hidden from the companion preview. One per account/week, with cached-screen grace and no missed-week penalty, extra XP or ranking benefit. Requests alone never award keepsakes. This encourages watching the game but cannot prove playlist inclusion or hardware display; TRMNL’s own dashboard preview can also reveal the code. No guaranteed Creator Fund revenue is assumed.

Review current fund rules and costs monthly. Any future paid tier, broader platform support or inactivity/progression policy requires a separate explicit product decision.
